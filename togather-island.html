Game Overview

ToGather:Island is an open-world survival game developed by Studio Kamii and Todak Studios and published by Todak Studios.

View on Steam →

ToGather:Island is an open-world survival adventure developed by Todak Studios, set across a science-fantasy island environment. The game combines exploration, resource gathering, crafting, building, combat, survival mechanics, and cinematic storytelling.

My involvement focused on the visual and cinematic side of real-time production, contributing to VFX, character animation, in-game cinematics, and cutscenes.

My Role

✨ Real-Time VFX

Created and integrated real-time visual effects for gameplay and cinematic moments, working with Unreal Engine's real-time VFX workflow.

🎭 Character Animation

Worked with character assets in Blender to create and refine animation content for gameplay and cinematic sequences before integrating it into the Unreal Engine workflow.

🎬 Cinematics & Cutscenes

Produced in-game cinematic sequences and cutscenes using Unreal Engine, combining Sequencer, cameras, animation, VFX, and environments.

🎥 Camera & Presentation

Worked on camera composition, movement, timing, and cinematic presentation to support gameplay moments and in-game storytelling.

Technical Art Workflow

My work connected 3D asset preparation, animation, VFX and real-time engine implementation across multiple stages of the production workflow.
