Game Overview

Police Sentri is an online multiplayer game inspired by the classic police-and-robbers and hide-and-seek concepts. Players take the role of police or robbers, with up to 10 players able to join an online session.

The game is published by Todak Studios Sdn. Bhd. and is available on Google Play.

My Role

My role on Police Sentri focused primarily on game development DevOps and release workflow. I worked with the development team using GitHub to manage project changes and support the preparation of mobile builds.

I was involved across the workflow from development and build preparation through testing and platform deployment, helping move the game toward published Android and iOS releases.
