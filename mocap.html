My Role

Worked on motion capture and virtual production setups that connected physical performers and live cameras with Unreal Engine in real time. My role involved configuring the tracking pipeline, establishing communication between capture software and Unreal Engine, and preparing the live camera workflow for combining real-world footage with virtual environments.

The goal was to create a reliable real-time pipeline where body movement could drive a digital character while the live camera feed could be combined with Unreal Engine's virtual environment for virtual production.

Motion Capture Pipeline

📡 OptiTrack Motive

Set up OptiTrack Motive to receive and process motion capture data from the tracking system, including body tracking and performer movement.

🔗 Motive → Unreal Engine

Configured the communication pipeline between Motive and Unreal Engine so real-time tracking data could be transmitted into the engine and used to drive digital characters.

🕺 Real-Time Body Tracking

Integrated live body-tracking data into Unreal Engine, allowing performer movement to be reflected by a digital character in real time for interactive and virtual production workflows.

🎥 Tracking & Calibration

Worked on the setup and calibration of the tracking environment to ensure the captured movement and virtual representation aligned correctly during real-time production.

Live Camera & Virtual Environment

I also worked with Blackmagic Design equipment to bring a live camera feed into the production workflow. The camera signal was prepared so real-world footage could be combined with the Unreal Engine environment.

This allowed the production setup to merge the physical camera image with digitally generated environments, creating a real-time bridge between the real and virtual worlds.
